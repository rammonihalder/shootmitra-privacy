Privacy Policy for ShootMitra

Developer: Hridita Innovations
Package name: com.hriditainnovations.shootmitra
Effective date: 12 August 2026

Hridita Innovations built ShootMitra as a photography reference, media organization, and workflow utility application. This Privacy Policy explains how ShootMitra handles information and device access when you use the app.

1. Personal Information

ShootMitra does not require users to create an account and does not ask users to provide personal information such as name, email address, phone number, or location for normal app use.

2. Photos, Videos and Storage Access

ShootMitra allows users to select, view, organize, and manage photography reference photos and videos. Depending on the Android version and feature being used, the app may use Android's system photo picker, document picker, or storage access available on older Android versions.

Media access is used only to provide the features requested by the user. ShootMitra does not automatically upload the user's photos or videos to Hridita Innovations.

3. Local Data Storage

ShootMitra stores app data locally on the user's device. Saved categories, media references, notes, settings, and other app-related information remain on the device unless the user intentionally exports, shares, deletes, or otherwise manages that information using available app or Android features.

4. Data Collection and Sharing

ShootMitra does not collect or transmit the user's locally stored photos, videos, personal media library, or app content to Hridita Innovations servers. Hridita Innovations does not sell or rent user data.

If the user chooses to share content through Android's system sharing features or opens a third-party website or application, that action is initiated by the user. The privacy practices of the receiving service are governed by that service's own privacy policy.

5. External Links

ShootMitra may allow users to save or open links to websites or third-party services. Hridita Innovations is not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of third-party websites or applications.

6. Permissions

ShootMitra may request device permissions that are necessary for app functionality, including access related to media or storage on supported Android versions. Users can deny or revoke permissions through Android settings. Some features may not work correctly if a required permission is denied.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because ShootMitra does not maintain a server-side user account or cloud database for the user's app content, there is no remote account data to delete. Locally stored app data may be removed by deleting content inside the app, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app, subject to Android system behavior and any backups created by the user.

8. Security

ShootMitra is designed so that user content remains primarily under the user's control on their own device. Users are encouraged to use Android security features such as a screen lock and device encryption to protect locally stored information.

9. Children's Privacy

ShootMitra is a general photography utility application and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not intentionally operate a remote system for collecting personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ShootMitra's features, permissions, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be made available on this page.
